# Generative AI for everyone

A non-technical introduction to generative AI: what it can and can't do, how projects are built, and what it means for work and business.

**Roles:** everyone, especially product managers, business leaders and anyone new to AI.
**Prerequisites:** none

## Key concepts

- **What it is.** Generative AI produces text, images, audio and code. LLMs generate text by repeatedly predicting the next word, which lets them write, summarize, answer and reason over text.
- **What it's good at:** writing (drafts, rewrites, translation), reading (summarizing, extracting, classifying) and chatting (assistants, support).
- **What it's bad at:** facts it wasn't given (it can confidently make things up), up-to-date knowledge, exact math, and anything where a wrong answer is costly without review.
- **How projects get built.** Start with a prompt, evaluate, then improve with better prompts, retrieval of your own documents (RAG) or fine-tuning, and keep a human in the loop where it matters.
- **Think in tasks, not jobs.** Break roles into tasks and ask which tasks AI can augment or automate. That's where the value is, and most jobs change rather than disappear.
- **Use it responsibly:** be transparent about AI use, protect data, check for bias, and decide who is accountable for outputs; see [AI governance](../../08-evaluation-and-safety/ai-governance/).
